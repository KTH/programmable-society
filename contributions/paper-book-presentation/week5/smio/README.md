## Description

Safe Browsing was first introduce in order to counter human mistakes. That is, accessing resources that would lead to financial, or data loss. The authors of this paper introduced this concept to blockchain, in order to protects users from unsafe contracts, and to incetivize the discovery and sharing of bugs in contracts.


**Relevance**

Saving users from losing billions of dollars.
