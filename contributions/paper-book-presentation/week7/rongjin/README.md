## Description

This presentation will explore the concept of Honeypots scam in Ethereum smart contract field. Unlike traditional exploits where attackers target vulnerable code, honeypots are predatory contracts deployed by scammers. They appear to have obvious flaws to lure in victims, only to trap the funds when the victim attempts to exploit the "flaw." The review covers the paper’s three main contributions: a taxonomy of honeypot techniques, the development of the detection tool HoneyBadger, and a large-scale analysis of the Ethereum network.

**Relevance**

This topic directly relates to the ethics of the blockchain, discussing the idea of "the weakest part in information security is us the humans."
