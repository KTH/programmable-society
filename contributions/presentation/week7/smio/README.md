## Description

I will present the concept of Dynamic NFT's (DNFT) that evolve automatically based on real-world data. Unlike standard ERC-721 tokens which point to static IPFS metadata, dNFTs utilize Chainlink Oracles and Automation (Keepers) to update their appearance and properties on-chain without human intervention.

**Relevance**

Most "Smart Contracts for Art" discussions stop at minting and selling. The concept of DNFT's bring closer the 2 worlds and creates a new way in which digital art can shine, and can become more valuable than physical art.
