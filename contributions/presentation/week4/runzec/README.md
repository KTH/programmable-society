## Description

Presenting NFTs starting from their background and basic concepts, then explaining how standards ensure on-chain uniqueness, how metadata and tokenURI operate, and how ownership is verified. 

The presentation also shows how these technical foundations enable high-value digital collectibles such as Bored Ape Yacht Club and Azuki, where verifiable provenance and enforced scarcity support the commercialization of premium digital artworks.

## **Relevance**

NFTs demonstrate programmable digital ownership and represent one of the most influential smart contract applications in modern blockchain ecosystems.
