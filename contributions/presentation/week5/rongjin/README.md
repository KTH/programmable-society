## Description

I will present the paradox behind the world's largest sized stable coin Tether USDT's ERC-20 smart contract, mainly covering the introduction of USDT, its 2017-era architecture and smart contract implementation, its security features and concerns in code level and real-world regulation level.

**Relevance**

USDT is not how the OG Crypto Community pictured the use of blockchain, but today it is one of the most critical cryptocurrencies in the industry and a on-chain future. With 184 billion USDs highly rely on this smart contract, it's worth our interests in how it's secured as well as how the holders' asset may not be fully secured under the centralized nature.
