## Description

This presentation will explore the evolution of consensus mechanisms in blockchain technology, specifically focusing on the transition from Proof of Work (PoW) to Proof of Stake (PoS).

It will cover the following key points:

- Brief overview of blockchain consensus mechanisms
- Why consensus is necessary in decentralized systems
- State the focus: transition from PoW to PoS

**Relevance**

The shift from PoW to PoS has significant implications for the philosophy and ethics of technology, particularly in terms of energy consumption, environmental impact, and the democratization of access to blockchain networks.
