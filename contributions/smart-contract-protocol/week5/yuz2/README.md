## Description  
This project develops the Decentralized Family Registry (DFR) Protocol, an original, security-hardened smart contract to manage marital relationships and shared finances on the blockchain. The protocol is built on three core pillars:

- Dual-Authorization Lifecycle: Both Marriage Registration and Divorce Finalization strictly require cryptographic consent from both parties.

- Joint Asset Custody: Manages a co-owned fund (jointBalance) where deposits are free, but all withdrawals are secured by dual signatures.

- Permanent Family Records: Stores immutable records of events (e.g., Child Registration), tying them to a specific, unique marriageId for data integrity.

Upon divorce, the protocol automatically executes the equitable distribution (50/50 split) of all locked joint assets, replacing centralized bureaucracy with automated, verifiable self-execution.

**Relevance**  
The DFR demonstrates how blockchain can transform traditionally slow and centralized legal processes—like family records and asset division—into a transparent, secure, and self-executing system. It sets a benchmark for trusted, enforceable digital agreements.
