## Title
Future-Proofing Blockchain Infrastructure in the Quantum Era: Quantum Blind Signatures for Secure Smart Contracts

## Names and KTH ID

## Category
- Paper Presentation
- Reference Paper: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2214212620308255 (2021)

## Description
This presentation explores how emerging quantum technologies will shape the foundational infrastructure of blockchain systems, with a focus on smart contract platforms in healthcare. The reference paper introduces a novel hybrid architecture that integrates quantum blind signatures directly into the transaction and authentication layers of a blockchain, demonstrating a blueprint for quantum-resilient infrastructure.

## Relevance
As the threat landscape shifts with advances in quantum computing, the core infrastructure of blockchain systems must evolve to remain secure and reliable, not just for healthcare blockchains, but for any sector relying on the robustness, privacy, and scalability of blockchain technology in the coming quantum era.
